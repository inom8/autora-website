The Problem

THE MARKET IS BROKEN

Every day, thousands of drivers in Uzbekistan face unexpected car problems with no reliable digital solution. Finding a trusted service, booking an appointment, or calling a towing truck is still fragmented, opaque, and stressful.

01

No Digital Discovery

Drivers find mechanics through word-of-mouth or random roadside stops. Zero online presence, zero trust.

02

Zero Price Transparency

No way to compare prices before committing. Hidden costs and unexpected bills are the norm.

03

Unreliable Emergency Response

Towing services have no digital dispatch. Response times are unpredictable and prices are arbitrary.

04

No Accountability

No rating systems, no reviews. Drivers have no way to assess service quality before it's too late.
